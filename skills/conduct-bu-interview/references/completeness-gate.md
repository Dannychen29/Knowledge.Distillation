# Completeness gate

The package is ready for distillation only when:

- the requirement and expected outcome are confirmed;
- the ordinary flow is traceable from trigger to outcome;
- material actors, inputs, outputs, handoffs and systems are identified;
- high-impact decisions include cues and rationale or an explicit unresolved owner;
- material exceptions and escalation paths are captured or accepted as limitations;
- every development-relevant step has enough evidence to identify its source, destination, data object, required fields, validation and completion proof, or a named `development_blocker` records the missing detail;
- spoken-only workflow descriptions are not treated as observed screen actions, field mappings, downloads, uploads or completion states;
- requested evidence is received, declined or recorded as unavailable;
- every requested walkthrough has a validated transcript or an explicit audio limitation, and any material gap exposed by that transcript has been followed up;
- contradictions and uncertainty remain visible;
- the participant confirms the summary.

Completeness means sufficient for the agreed task, not exhaustive documentation of the business.

## Development blocker test

Create or keep a material gap open when any of these are missing for a workflow that may become software, automation, a guided UI, a form filler, an integration, or a structured review workbook:

- exact source system or location for an input;
- acquisition method, including download, export, query, email, shared folder, API or manual receipt;
- required source fields and target fields;
- transformation, reconciliation or validation rule;
- exact upload, delivery or storage destination;
- output format, template version, recipient, acceptance check or proof of completion;
- decision input provenance, freshness, rationale or missing/conflicting-input behavior.

For each blocker, write the smallest useful follow-up: one targeted question, one existing artifact, one redacted example, one screenshot set, or one short screen walkthrough. Do not ask for a full recording when a smaller artifact resolves the blocker.
